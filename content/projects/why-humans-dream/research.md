# Research handoff — Why humans dream

This is a placeholder research handoff for the first pipeline demonstration.

Before publication, replace this file with a source-backed research brief.
The production rule is simple: **do not treat this draft as verified research.**

## Questions to investigate

1. What happens during REM sleep?
2. Do dreams occur outside REM sleep?
3. Why are dreams often emotional or strange?
4. What evidence exists for memory and emotional processing during sleep?
5. Which dream theories are well supported, and which remain hypotheses?
6. Why do people forget most dreams so quickly?

## Editorial guardrails

- Distinguish observed sleep physiology from theories about dream function.
- Do not claim that dreams have one universally accepted purpose.
- Avoid presenting dream interpretation as scientific fact.
